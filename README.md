# Group Project
Names:
Thomas, William, Jonah, and Ronan. 
## Alchemist's Crypt 
In this game the player will explore the map, acquire random abilities, and fight enemies.
## Style/theme
This game will be a top down deckbuilder
## Who might consider playing this game:
Those who like rogue-lite games like Dead cells, The Binding of Isaac, and Vampire Survivors.
## Main action or control:
Includes walking, running, and firing projectiles. 
## Feedback/Challenge:
The player has to battle enemies of increasing difficulty, and builds a deck as they defeat them.
## Player's goal: 
Explore different rooms, collect upgrades to make the character more powerful, and defeat the boss to complete the game.
